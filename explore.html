Featured Destinations

Discover some of the key tourism and heritage sites in Somalia.

HERITAGE

Laas Geel

Laas Geel is famed for its ancient rock paintings and archaeological treasures. This site provides valuable insight in to the area's ancient past and rich cultural history.

CITY & COAST

Mogadishu

Mogadishu, the capital city of Somalia, has a fascinating history that is closely tied to trade, culture and the Indian Ocean.

COASTAL TOURISM

Kismayo

The coastal city of Kismayo is closely linked to beaches, the ocean and natural attractions in the surrounding area. Tourism has the potential to support numerous local businesses and communities.

COASTAL

Berbera

Berbera is an important coastal trading hub with a rich and varied history that is connected to maritime trade. The area has the potential to become a major tourism destination due to its coastline.

HISTORY

Zeila

Zeila is a historic coastal settlement with a rich history that is connected to trade and regional faiths. The area has the potential to be a significant tourism destination for cultural and heritage-based activities.

CULTURE

Somali Culture

Somali culture includes poetry, music, cuisine, traditional dress, crafts and hospitality. Tourism has the potential to safeguard these valued traditions.
