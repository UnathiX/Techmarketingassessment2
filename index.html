Somalia at a Glance

Discover some of the features that make Somalia an interesting tourism destination.

Coastline

Somalia has a long coastline along the Indian Ocean and Gulf of Aden.

Culture

Somali culture includes traditional music, food, clothing, poetry and hospitality.

Heritage

Somalia contains archaeological and historical sites such as Laas Geel.

Nature

The country has beaches, marine environments, wildlife and diverse landscapes.
