Sustainable Somalia

Tourism that supports people, culture and the environment.

SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Sustainable tourism can create economic opportunities and help promote local culture and products.

How tourism can contribute


1. Local employment
Tourism businesses can create jobs in the local area, including the hospitality, transport, guiding, catering and other sectors.


2. Local businesses
Visitors can support locally owned restaurants, independent businesses, accommodation providers and tour operators.


3. Cultural heritage
Tourism can create incentives to preserve and promote cultural heritage where managed responsibly.


4. Entrepreneurship
Young people and local entrepreneurs can develop tourism related products and services.

Community-Based Tourism

Tourism should create opportunities for the communities where visitors spend their time.

Community Jobs

Tourism can create opportunities in guiding, hospitality, transport, food services and cultural activities.

Local Businesses

Tourists can support locally owned shops, restaurants, craftspeople and accommodation providers.

Women & Youth

Tourism entrepreneurship can provide opportunities for women and young people to participate in the economy.

Protecting the Environment

Sustainable tourism should reduce negative environmental impacts and protect natural resources.

Protect Coastal Areas

Encourage responsible behaviour around beaches, marine environments and coastal communities.

Reduce Waste

Tourism businesses and visitors can reduce plastic waste and encourage recycling and responsible disposal.

Save Resources

Hotels and tourism businesses can use water and energy efficiently.

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

Responsible tourism encourages visitors and businesses to use resources carefully, reduce waste and make responsible purchasing decisions.


Supporting local products can also keep more tourism income within local communities.
